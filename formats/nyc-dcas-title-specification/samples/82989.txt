THE MANAGERIAL SERVICE \[061\] FLSA CODE

ADMINISTRATIVE PUBLIC HEALTH SANITARIAN E

Duties and Responsibilities

This is a management class of positions with several assignment levels. Under general direction, directs the performance of difficult and responsible work in the inspection of food service operations, the drinking water supply, environmental lead hazards, pest control, recreational bathing facilities, day camps, other commercial facilities and private and multiple dwellings to ensure compliance with safety and health standards. Directs organizational segments of the Department of Health responsible for the enforcement of the N.Y.C. Health Code. All incumbents perform related work. The following are typical assignments within this class of positions.

Under managerial direction, performs highly difficult and responsible work relating to the administration of large organizational segments in the Department of Health involved in inspections to improve food, drug, general environmental sanitation, safety and health standards and to assure compliance with pertinent laws, rules and regulations governing these areas of activity. Develops procedures to enforce the Health Code and to implement inspection policies and objectives. Makes recommendations for new or revised legislation, or for revisions, deletions or additions to the Health Code. Directs the planning of an in-service training program and educational programs such as those for food plant operators and employees. Initiates and directs the conduct of special surveys and research projects. Plans emergency programs to meet environmental sanitation problems caused by strikes, floods, fuel shortages, etc. Reviews and takes appropriate action on reports submitted by subordinates; prepares periodic reports and makes recommendations on ongoing activities. Interprets policies and programs to the public through lectures and news releases. Confers with and advises other organizational segments, other governmental agencies, and representatives of the private sector. Prepares annual budget requests. Acts as advisor to superiors on inspectional matters; or performs other assignments equivalent to those described herein.

Qualification Requirements

1\. A baccalaureate degree from an accredited college, including or supplemented by 30 credits in the biological and/or physical sciences, and four years of satisfactory full-time experience performing inspections to assure compliance with pertinent laws, rules and regulations governing the areas of food, drugs, and general environmental sanitation, eighteen months of which must have been in an administrative, managerial or executive capacity, or in supervising a staff performing food, drug, and general environmental sanitation inspections or related work: or

2\. An associate degree from an accredited college, including or supplemented by 12 credits in the biological and/or physical sciences, and six years of satisfactory full-time experience as described in "1" above.
